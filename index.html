World Explorer VR

"Distance should not determine whether someone gets to experience the world."
A fully immersive, accessible way to visit landmarks, wonders and cultures — no flights, no stairs, no barriers.
How it works:
  • Choose a destination from the floating World Map.
  • Look around freely — everything is designed to be viewed comfortably while seated.
  • Point at glowing ℹ️ markers to learn about each place.
  • Point at "Return to World Map" any time to pick a new destination.
🥽 On Meta Quest 3S: point either controller and pull the trigger to select a menu card or an info marker. Point the controller at the floor and pull the trigger to teleport there instantly — no physical walking required.
🖥️ On laptop / desktop: click and drag the mouse to look around. Use W A S D or the arrow keys to walk. Click any menu card, floor tile, or ℹ️ marker to select it.
Accessibility: Every interaction here uses simple point‑and‑select — no reaching, crouching, running, or fine motor precision is required. The whole experience works seated.
This browser/device doesn't report VR support — you can still explore on desktop, or open this page in the Meta Quest Browser.
VR: point & pull the trigger to select or teleport. Desktop: WASD/arrows to walk, click to select.
